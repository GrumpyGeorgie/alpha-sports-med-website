## Distinct angle for this page

**The endurance and running physio page.**

The Ascot Vale GBP service list carries two entries that appear at no other Alpha site: **Triathlon & Endurance Athlete Physiotherapy** and **Running Injury Assessment** delivered by a "running expert" practitioner. Ascot Vale is also the clinic tied to the running cluster — Running Assessment, RunWest Run Club, the running blog content. That is a real, documented, site-specific specialisation and it is the whole differentiation of this page.

So: this is physiotherapy for people who run, ride, swim and train, written for someone with a race in eight weeks who has been told to stop. The Bacchus Marsh physio page is work-and-club; Newport is depth-of-team; this one is endurance. The three must not converge.

The playbook's "rest isn't the whole answer" position is the argument to make here, carefully — load management and a tailored return, not blanket rest. Avoid the word "performance" throughout (founder-flagged). Use "getting back to what you love" and "the goal you're training for".

## AHPRA Compliance Notes

The "rest isn't the whole answer" position is the sharpest AHPRA edge in this brief. It is defensible as a described clinical philosophy; it is not defensible as advice to a reader who hasn't been assessed. Frame it as how Alpha approaches assessment ("we look at load before we look at stopping"), never as a directive ("you don't need to stop running"). No implied guarantee of making an event or race date — that is an outcome promise in a particularly seductive form. Anna Lawther's credentials and AHPRA registration must be accurate; "special interest in endurance athletes" is fine, "specialist" is not.
