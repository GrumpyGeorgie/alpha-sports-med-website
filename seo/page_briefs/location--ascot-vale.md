## AHPRA Compliance Notes

The clinic pages are the highest-risk pages in this batch for comparative claims, because the natural instinct when writing a location page is to say why you're better than the other clinics on the same street. Do not. No "the best osteopath in Ascot Vale", no "Ascot Vale's leading clinic", no implied ranking. Practitioner credentials must be stated accurately with AHPRA registration numbers and nothing inflated — "special interest in running injuries" is fine, "specialist" is a protected title and is not. The Maidstone origin story is safe as long as it stays factual and doesn't imply superior outcomes from longevity.
