## Reader

Someone in Newport, Williamstown, Spotswood or Altona North who has decided they want a physio — a specific injury, often running or group-fitness related. High-income, health-literate catchment. They want to know they'll see a real physiotherapist who gets active people, and that the wider team is there if they need it.

## Search Intent

Commercial, suburb-qualified, local-pack heavy. Below-pack organic plus GBP relevance. The page converts on: a named physiotherapist, honest first-appointment detail, and the multidisciplinary backup.

## The honesty constraint

**Newport is staffed osteo-heavy (roughly seven osteopaths to one physiotherapist, Anna Lawther).** The page must own the physio term for search without overselling physio capacity. The right framing: you came for a physio, here's our physio and how she works, and here's the full team she works alongside when your care would benefit from it. Do not imply a large physio department. Do not fabricate additional physios.
