## Purpose

Convert chiropractic searchers by presenting an evidence-informed, musculoskeletal chiropractic practice that sits inside a multidisciplinary team — while reassuring the reader who is nervous about what chiropractic involves.

## Reader

The most polarised audience on the site, split three ways. (1) The **convert** — has seen a chiro before, wants an adjustment, is simply looking for one nearby. (2) The **sceptic** — has heard chiropractic is dubious, is here reluctantly because someone recommended it, and is scanning for signs of legitimacy or otherwise. (3) The **nervous** — worried about neck manipulation specifically, has read something alarming, and needs the safety question answered before anything else.

Bacchus Marsh is the chiropractic-lead clinic (chiropractor bacchus marsh 320), so a meaningful share of this traffic is outer-west.

Five-second need: this is a registered, five-year-university-trained profession, practised here as musculoskeletal care inside a wider team — and nobody will do anything to you that you haven't agreed to.

## Search Intent

Commercial with an unusually heavy trust burden. `chiropractor near me` (40,500, competition 0.56) is the highest-competition term in the modality set. ## AHPRA Compliance Notes

**Highest-risk service page on the site.** Chiropractic advertising is actively monitored and has been the subject of specific AHPRA and Chiropractic Board guidance.

- **Prohibited framing:** no claims that chiropractic treats or influences non-musculoskeletal conditions — no asthma, colic, immunity, ear infections, ADHD, bedwetting, "nervous system function" or general wellness claims.
- **No spinal X-ray screening** promotion for asymptomatic patients.
- Do not use "subluxation", "realignment", "misalignment", "nerve interference", "innate".
- No maintenance-care or ongoing-wellness-plan marketing that implies necessity without clinical indication.
- No paediatric chiropractic claims. If paediatric care is offered at all, it must not appear on this page without express clinical and compliance review — the site's paediatric offering is osteopathic and lives on its own page.
- No comparative superiority over physiotherapy or osteopathy.
- Testimonials must not imply guaranteed results.
- The safety section must be accurate and balanced; understating risk is itself a compliance problem.
