## AHPRA Compliance Notes

Same elevated baseline as the Ascot Vale chiropractic brief — **chiropractic advertising is under active AHPRA enforcement attention.** Everything on this page stays inside musculoskeletal assessment and treatment. No wellness, maintenance-care, immunity, subluxation or general-health framing. No claims about adjustment producing effects beyond the musculoskeletal.

The training angle adds its own risk. "You can keep training" is advice to an unassessed reader and must not appear as a general statement. The compliant construction describes the clinic's approach ("we look at what you can keep doing rather than starting from stop") rather than instructing the reader. No timeline promises for return to lifting or sport — this is the specific outcome-guarantee trap for this page, and it is a tempting one because the reader wants exactly that number.

"Dr" must be used with the discipline evident, per Australian advertising convention.
