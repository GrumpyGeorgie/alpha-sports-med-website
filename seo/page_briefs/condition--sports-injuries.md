## Purpose

Own the head of the sports cluster (`sports injuries` 720 + `sports injury clinic` 590 + `sports injury physio` 320), route to the six child pages, and absorb the merged Gym Injuries and Soft Tissue Injuries pages without diluting into a catch-all.

## Reader

Injured in the last few days to few weeks, doing something they care about. Two dominant states:

- **Acute and frightened.** It happened on Saturday. There was a noise, or a lot of swelling, or they couldn't finish. They want to know if it's serious and what to do tonight.
- **Grumbling and frustrated.** It's been three weeks, it's not settling, and they've been managing it with ice and hope.

Both are asking a version of "is this bad, and who do I see". Neither wants a physiology lecture.

Five-second need: *this is a clinic that deals with this kind of injury all day, and I can get in.*

### 3. What to do in the first 48 hours
**Component:** `problem-statement` or content block
**Direction:** Highest-utility block on the page and the reason someone bookmarks it. Give genuinely useful acute advice, then the red flags. **Salvage `/education_hub/rest-is-not-always-best/` here** — its argument is exactly Alpha's positioning and it should become this section's spine rather than being discarded.
**Key points:**
- Current guidance in plain language: protect and offload early, then start gentle movement sooner than most people expect. Avoid reciting an acronym as if it were gospel.
- Ice: honest framing — helps comfort, evidence for healing is weak. Don't oversell it.
- The rest argument (salvaged): early protection yes, prolonged rest no. Tissue adapts to load; too long off and capacity drops.
- **Red flags → go to an emergency department or GP, not to us:** obvious deformity, inability to bear any weight, a joint that gave way completely, numbness or pins and needles below the injury, a limb that looks pale or cold, severe pain out of proportion, any head knock with the concussion warning signs → /conditions/sports-injuries/concussion/
- Keep this block scannable. Bullets, not paragraphs.
