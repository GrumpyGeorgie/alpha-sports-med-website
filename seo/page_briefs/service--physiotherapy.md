## Purpose

Own the sports physiotherapy term for Melbourne and convert the athlete or gym-goer who has been through generic physio before and wants someone who understands their training.

## Reader

The most self-aware reader on the site. Usually an active person — runner, lifter, F45/CrossFit member, weekend club player — who has already had physio somewhere. That is the crucial detail: the qualifier "sports" in the search means they are deliberately filtering out the physio who gave them a theraband and told them to stop running.

They are frustrated by exactly the things the playbook names: textbook treatment, "rest and see how it goes", and a plan that had nothing to do with their actual training load.

Five-second need: these people understand training, they won't just tell me to stop, and they'll get me back to it.

## Search Intent

Commercial, high qualification. `sports physio melbourne` (320) is a low-competition, high-intent term with a specific buyer. The enormous `physio near me` (90,500) volume is won through GBP and location pages, not here — but this page feeds that relevance.

The searcher's unspoken question is not "what is physiotherapy". It is "will you let me keep training?"

### 2. "Just rest it" is not a plan
**Component:** `problem-statement`
**Direction:** The page's strongest argument and its clearest point of difference. The playbook records this as the single most common piece of bad advice patients arrive with. Take a clear, defensible position: rest has its place, but complete rest deconditions the tissue you need to load, and a managed return beats stopping almost every time.
**Key points:**
- Name the experience: told to stop running for six weeks, came back, it hurt again in a fortnight
- Explain load in plain language — tissue adapts to what you ask of it, and the goal is to find the dose it can handle today, then build
- Be clinically responsible: acknowledge that some injuries genuinely require offloading, and that the difference is knowing which is which
- This is education, not a promise — keep it AHPRA-safe

### 4. How we work
**Component:** `approach-steps`
**Direction:** The differentiation section. Physiotherapy is the most commoditised term in the set — every clinic in Melbourne has a physio. The argument is the integration and the depth of assessment.
**Key points:**

- Assessment of the whole chain, plus strength and movement testing where relevant — numbers, so you can see progress instead of guessing

- Programming built around your week: your sessions, your race date, your kids' schedule
- The strength phase happens here, with exercise physiology, clinical pilates and S&C in the same building. Most clinics discharge at the point where the pain stops; that's the point where re-injury risk is highest.

## AHPRA Compliance Notes

- The load-management argument must be framed as an approach, not a superior outcome. "We work with you to find what your body can tolerate" — not "you'll recover faster with us".
- Do not state or imply that rest advice from other practitioners is wrong. Frame it as a default that doesn't suit every case.
- Medicare statements must specify that eligibility requires a GP-managed care plan and is limited in number.
- Return-to-sport timeframes must never be presented as guarantees.
- No "specialist" language without specialist registration — "sports physiotherapist" as a job description is fine; "sports physiotherapy specialist" is not unless titled.

## Voice Notes
