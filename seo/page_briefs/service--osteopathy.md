## Purpose

## Reader

Two arrivals. The first knows what an osteopath is, has probably seen one before, and is comparison shopping on trust and location. The second doesn't really know and is searching the word because someone recommended it — often after physio or massage didn't hold.

Five-second need: these are properly qualified osteopaths, they treat people like me, and I can book without a referral.

## AHPRA Compliance Notes

- **"Dr" usage:** every osteopath using the title must have "Osteopath" stated immediately alongside it.
- No claims that osteopathy cures, corrects or realigns anything. Describe what treatment involves and what patients commonly report, not what it will achieve.
- The fascial explanation must stay descriptive. Do not overstate the mechanism or present a contested model as settled fact.
- Manipulation must be described as consent-led and optional, never as a signature technique that fixes things.
- No comparative claims against physiotherapy or chiropractic — this is the highest-risk page for that because the FAQ invites the comparison.
- Testimonials, if used, cannot imply guaranteed results.
