## 3. How chiropractic works here

**H2:** How chiropractic works at Alpha

**Concept callout:** Consent-led care. Every technique is explained first, and every one is optional.

**Copy:**
Chiropractic is a registered health profession focused on the musculoskeletal system, particularly the spine and joints. In Australia, chiropractors are registered through AHPRA, complete five years of university training and keep their skills current through ongoing professional development. At Alpha, our chiropractors work on musculoskeletal pain and movement, as part of a wider team.

Every first visit starts with assessment: your history, how you move and some simple testing. Only then do we talk about treatment. Depending on what we find, that may include joint mobilisation, soft tissue work, exercise prescription and, where appropriate and you agree, spinal manipulation.

Manipulation is one option, not the whole service. We'll explain what it involves and why we're suggesting it, and you can say no. There are gentler alternatives, and plenty of effective care happens without it. Nothing happens that you haven't agreed to.

Your plan has clear goals and a point where we review progress together. As things improve, the focus shifts towards strength and load capacity, so you can manage well on your own rather than rely on regular treatment.

**For the designer:** The most important section on the page. Give it room. It must never read as criticism of other chiropractors.

## 7. Your first appointment

**H2:** Your first chiropractic appointment

**Intro:** A clear, conversational process with no surprises.

1. **Tell us what's going on.** We discuss your history, what you've tried and what you want to get back to.
2. **Move through an assessment.** Your chiropractor looks at how you move, runs some simple tests and explains what they notice.
3. **Agree on treatment.** Options are explained, including alternatives to manipulation, before any hands-on care begins.
4. **Leave with a plan.** You'll usually have exercises or advice for between visits, plus a clear point where we review progress.

**Come in comfortable clothes:** Wear something you can move in; activewear works well.
**Nothing without your say-so:** Tell us what feels okay. You can pause, change your mind or skip a technique at any point.
**Tell us your health history:** Let us know about osteoporosis, blood-thinning medication, recent injuries or any other changes to your health.

## 10. FAQs

**H2:** Chiropractic FAQs

**Are chiropractors registered, and is chiropractic safe?**
Yes, chiropractors in Australia must be registered with AHPRA, and you can check any practitioner on the public register. Chiropractic is generally low-risk when provided by a registered practitioner. Mild soreness or stiffness for a day or two is common. Serious complications, including those linked with neck manipulation, are rare but can occur, which is why we take a full history and screen before treatment. Neck manipulation is always discussed first, never required, and other options are available.

**Do I need a referral?**
No. You can book a chiropractor directly. If your GP has set you up with a chronic condition management plan, you may be eligible for some Medicare-subsidised visits, so it's worth mentioning when you book.

**Can I claim on private health?**
Chiropractic may be covered when your extras policy includes it. Check your level of cover with your fund before your visit.

**Does an adjustment hurt? What's the noise?**
Most people feel pressure rather than pain, and some feel a little sore afterwards. The click you sometimes hear is a pressure change in the fluid inside the joint, not a bone moving back into place. If you'd rather not have manipulation, just say so.

**Do I have to keep coming forever?**
No. Your plan has goals and a review point that we agree with you. As things improve, visits usually space out and the focus moves to exercises you can do yourself. We won't recommend ongoing treatment without a clinical reason.

**Can I be treated without manipulation?**
Yes. Manipulation is one option among many. Mobilisation, soft tissue work and exercise can be used instead, and you can change your mind at any point.

**What's the difference between a chiro, an osteo and a physio?**
All three are AHPRA-registered and the conditions they treat often overlap. The approaches differ in emphasis: chiropractic focuses closely on the spine and joints, osteopathy takes a broad hands-on view, and physiotherapy tends to focus on movement and exercise. At Alpha, we work as one team to help you find the right fit. There's more on this on our blog.

**Can I see a chiropractor while pregnant?**
Care can be adapted during pregnancy, taking your stage, history and comfort into account. Let us know you're pregnant when you book, and we'll coordinate with your maternity care team when needed.
