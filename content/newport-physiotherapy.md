## 4. How physio works at Alpha

**H2:** How we work

**Steps:**

**1. We listen first.**
Your first appointment is about understanding you and your goals, not just the sore bit. What do you want to get back to? What have you already tried?

**2. We find the root cause.**
We look above and below the painful area for the thing that's actually loading it.

**3. Treatment and a plan.**
Hands-on treatment and movement work on the day, plus a plan built around your training week.

**4. We build you back stronger.**
Feeling better is the start. We keep going until you're robust enough to stay that way.

**Close line:** We re-test as we go, so you can feel the progress.

**For the designer:** Four steps, scannable.

No referral needed, you can book directly. If you have a GP care plan (EPC) or a WorkCover/TAC claim, we can work with that, just mention it when you book.

45 minutes, long enough to assess properly and explain the plan.

**Do you see WorkCover and TAC patients?**
