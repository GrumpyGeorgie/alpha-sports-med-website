## 2. Why Alpha exists

**H2:** It didn't start with success

**Copy:**
Before Alpha, our founder Dr Ashton Wilson was so disheartened by his first job as an osteopath that he seriously looked into becoming a jellyfish keeper at SeaWorld. In his words:

> "I was so disheartened by my first experience as an osteo I applied to be a jellyfish keeper at SeaWorld. I felt so lost within this profession, like I didn't fit the mould. So I decided to make my own."

In 2017 he saw the gap in Melbourne's west between traditional allied health and real, exercise-based rehab. He didn't just want to fill it. He wanted to build the clinic he could never find for himself: a place where practitioners wore activewear, where hands-on treatment and the fitness world actually worked together, and where active people got back to doing what they love.

Dr Will Krithararis built his foundation in aged care and during his graduate year at Alpha, then went away to grow into a senior, leadership role. But he always wanted more:

> "3 years into my work as just an osteopath, I wanted more. I built the confidence and belief that I could do it, and from there Ashton and I began Maidstone first, which is now Ascot Vale."

Two founders, one shared promise, framed slightly differently. Ashton wants patients to feel seen and heard. Will wants them to feel empowered. Validation and empowerment: those are the two halves of the Alpha experience.

**For the designer:** This is the best copy on the page. The SeaWorld line should land as a laugh, then a point. Give the two quotes visual weight.

## 3. What frustrated us

**H2:** What we were determined to do differently

**Copy:**
We built Alpha because too much of healthcare felt like this:

- Textbook treatment that wasn't built around the actual person in the room.
- "Stop running, stop the gym, just rest" handed out as the default answer to everything.
- One-sided, modality-first thinking, where the tool mattered more than the problem.
- Clinics that didn't really listen.

Alpha is our answer to all four. We treat the person, not the protocol.

**For the designer:** A short, confident list. Keep it about systems and defaults, never about naming or knocking other clinics.

## 4. How that shows up in your appointment

**H2:** Values you can actually feel

**Copy:**

**Genuine care.**
Your first appointment isn't rushed, because listening properly takes time.

**Education.**
You'll leave understanding more about your body than when you came in, and we explain the plan before we ask you to commit to it.

**Integration.**
Hands-on treatment and exercise rehab are one plan, not two separate referrals. The person who treats you talks to the person who programmes for you.

**Community.**
We know our patients, and we know their clubs, gyms and races.

**Authenticity.**
Activewear, not scrubs. No scripts. Just people who take the work seriously without taking themselves too seriously.

And every session ends with a re-test, so you can feel the change for yourself.

**For the designer:** Each value pinned to a concrete practice, otherwise the page is just vibes. Five short blocks.

## 5. What we won't do

**H2:** A few things we'll never do

**Copy:**
- We won't promise a quick fix.
- We won't discharge you the moment the pain settles, then leave you to it.
- We won't tell you to just rest and hope.
- We won't compete on price, and we're always happy to explain why our fees are what they are.

**For the designer:** Confident and calm, never defensive. This block pre-qualifies the right patient, which is exactly the point.
