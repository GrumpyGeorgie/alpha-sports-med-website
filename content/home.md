## 4. How we work

**H2:** How we work

**Intro line:** Four things you can expect, every time.

**Steps:**

**1. We listen first.**
Your first appointment is about understanding you and your goals, not just the sore bit. What do you want to get back to? What have you already tried?

**2. We find the root cause.**
We look above and below the painful area for the thing that's actually loading it. Often the spot that hurts isn't the spot causing it.

**3. Treatment and a plan.**
Hands-on treatment on the day, plus a plan built around your training, your week and your goal. Not a generic photocopied sheet.

**4. We build you back stronger.**
Feeling better is the start, not the finish. We keep going until you're robust enough to stay that way and get on with what you love.

**Close line:** We re-test at the end of every session, so you can feel the progress before you leave.

**For the designer:** Four steps, each concrete enough to picture the appointment. Numbered, scannable.

## Notes for review

**The angle: health/goal-led, not problem-led**
- Earlier draft opened on the pain ("the pain keeps coming back"). This version leads with the life the patient wants back ("get back to doing what you love"), keeps a single short beat of empathy, and treats root cause as the method rather than the headline. It's warmer, more on-brand for a premium active clinic, and still gives the frustrated reader the recognition that makes them book.
- Kept your loved eyebrow "Start your journey to better health" and the warm, genuine-care voice you already have.
- Kept the "not sure who to book with" section (answers the modality-confusion pain point) and the integrated-team framing.
- The word "performance" is deliberately avoided throughout (per Ashton's note).

**Placeholders / to confirm before publishing**

**AHPRA notes**
- No outcome guarantees anywhere. "We find out why" and "we re-test every session" are process claims and are safe. "Results actually last" describes the aim of the approach, not a promised result, keep it framed that way.
- If a testimonial or star rating is added, it must not imply guaranteed results, and needs written consent.
- No before/after imagery.
